# PULS media rights policy
Prefer CC0 and original project artwork. Allow CC BY and CC BY-SA only with attribution, original source, exact license and change indication. Adapted ShareAlike media remains under the original license; no DRM or claim of exclusive rights in third-party media. Public-domain material needs jurisdiction/basis review before release. Do not use NC/ND, unknown rights, screenshots from commercial sites, or files without provenance. Wikimedia metadata is not a legal warranty. Check trademarks/packaging rights, identifiable persons, embedded audio, privacy, source authenticity and clinical accuracy independently.
QA build: verify label and URL, title, credits, original/derived hashes, size, exact local inventory and APK contents. Pending visual/rights review is allowed only in QA; first-aid procedure media always needs clinical and rights approval. Run python3 tools/audit_media_rights.py --mode release to enforce the stricter gate before distributing a production build. Do not release QA artifacts to users as a finished product. Source links and attribution remain available locally via More > Media credits. This does not replace qualified legal/clinical review.
Official sources: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ ; https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ ; https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Non-copyright_restrictions .

Production Gradle preReleaseBuild and mergeReleaseAssets now depend on the stricter release audit. No release-review.json is supplied: production is intentionally blocked until independent copyright, medical and trademark/privacy review references exist. QA debug build is not a release. This gate cannot certify reviewer authenticity or all legal rights; records require human verification. Attribution and restriction notices from Commons are retained for review.
